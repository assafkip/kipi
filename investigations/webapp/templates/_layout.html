New here? Here's how it works.

An investigation runs in 4 steps. You don't need every button in the sidebar — just follow the highlighted Next step and it walks you through.

  1. 1Intake — drop your evidence: PDFs, screenshots, a scrape, or paste notes.
  2. 2Investigate — the agent digs the open web and builds the graph (it models the case for you — nothing to set up).
  3. 3Deliver — get the written brief.
  4. 4Portfolio — see how this case links to your others.
