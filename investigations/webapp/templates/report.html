Methodology & Provenance

Findings are derived from {{ d.stats.reports }} source report{{ '' if d.stats.reports == 1 else 's' }} ingested into this case. Entities and relationships are extracted automatically, then reviewed by an analyst. Attention scoring is a rank-by-priority signal (role, cross-report presence, network centrality, and analyst-flagged priors), not a maliciousness rating.
